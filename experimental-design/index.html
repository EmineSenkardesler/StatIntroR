Experimental design, step by step

How completely randomized, block, Latin square and split-plot layouts are built and why, using the data, seeds and layouts from the Week 13 pre-lecture.

Step 1 of 10

    Data: ratweight.CRD.csv and cycad_RCBD.csv from the pre-lecture, cochran.latin and stroup.splitplot from agridat, RBPottery from archdata, and Crossover_Latin.csv from the workshop. Layouts come from agricolae with the pre-lecture's seeds. ANOVA numbers come from lm() on these files.

    The fern treatments are coded 1, 2 and 3 in the file. They are labeled water, spores and oil here, in the order the pre-lecture lists them.
